What We Intentionally DON'T Do

Saying this clearly makes judges trust you more.

❌ No Call Tracking / Blocking
• Needs READ_CALL_LOG permission — Google Play rejects new apps for it
• Needs 1B number DB like Truecaller — we don't have
• Privacy risk — we refuse to upload your call history
Instead: You give us transcript of a suspicious call, we analyze it. No background tracking.
❌ No Auto SMS Filtering
Same permission issue. Real-time SMS filtering needs SMS Retriever access — blocked now. We do manual paste check — more private, still effective.
❌ No 40-Point Website Reputation
McAfee / Trend Micro use paid APIs for domain age, SSL, typosquat detection. We do basic check: is it .gov.in / .nic.in or fake? + short-link detection. For full check we link you to official gov.in.
🧠 Current Limitation: Keyword + Scoring
Today detection is fixed keyword list (English/Tamil/Tanglish). Clever scammer who rephrases can bypass. Clean result = NOT proof safe. Roadmap: Add Gemini Flash AI layer for true intent understanding — then we truly become AI Assistant.
